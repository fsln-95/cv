Profile

I'm a physicist working on the theory and numerical simulation of quantum dynamics in complex, disordered systems — decoherence, transport, and information scrambling, mostly probed through Nuclear Magnetic Resonance. My work is primarily computational: large-scale numerical simulations run on HPC clusters, paired with analytical models that guide and interpret experiments carried out by collaborators.

I did my undergraduate and Master's training at Instituto Balseiro, whose lab-intensive curriculum in electronics and instrumentation still shapes how I think about measurement. I'm currently a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Florence, contributing modeling and simulation work to APACE, an EU-funded, multi-site collaboration spanning academic and industry partners across Europe.
